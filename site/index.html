Twitch VOD → clips  ·  CUNY Tech Prep

Turn a long Twitch VOD into clips worth posting.

AutoClipper reads the chat and the audio, ranks the moments that popped, and hands back the top 10 as captioned 9:16 clips, or one 16:9 highlights video for anyone who missed the stream.

How it works

Chat explodes right after something happens. We listen for it.

  1. 01

    Read the signals

    Give it a VOD link. It pulls the chat replay, the audio and the clips viewers already made, then measures chat rate, laugh reactions and loudness for every few seconds of stream.

    chat rateLUL / KEKWloudness
  2. 02

    Score every moment

    A model trained on viewer clips (logistic regression, then LightGBM) scores each window, and has to beat simple baselines like "biggest chat spike" to earn its keep. Each pick comes with a one-line reason.

    ranked top 10why this moment
  3. 03

    Cut, caption, post

    The top moments come back as vertical 9:16 clips with burned-in captions. Nudge the start or end, download, or join them into one 16:9 highlights video to watch.

    9:16 clipscaptions16:9 highlights

Get it

Runs on your machine. One command to start.

AutoClipper runs locally: the app opens in your browser and your VODs stay on your computer. You need uv, Node.js 24 and git. The script fetches FFmpeg and the other tools for you, and the setup guide shows where your Twitch API keys go.

Pre-alpha. The full pipeline works end to end; models trained on real Twitch data are still in progress.

Open the repo ↗ Full setup guide

  1. 1 Clone the repo

    git clone https://github.com/adillpickles/AutoClipper.git
cd AutoClipper

  2. 2 Start it, on Windows (PowerShell)

    powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/demo.ps1

  3. 2 or on macOS / Linux

    bash scripts/demo.sh
