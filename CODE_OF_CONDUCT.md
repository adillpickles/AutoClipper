# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, including in code review
- Accepting responsibility for our mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people working on it

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

### Project-specific expectations

This project handles content and data created by streamers and their communities. In addition to the above:

- Don't use project data or tooling to target, mock or harass any streamer, chatter or creator.
- Don't publish clips, chat excerpts or usernames gathered by this project outside the rules in [`docs/data/data-governance.md`](docs/data/data-governance.md).

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct. They will explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and team chat. It also applies when someone is officially representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer through their GitHub profile ([@adillpickles](https://github.com/adillpickles)) or through the team's private channel. If the issue involves the maintainer, contact the program staff overseeing the project. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter will be respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction:** a private, written explanation of what was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning:** a warning with consequences for continued behavior, plus a period with no interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban:** a temporary ban from interaction or public communication with the project.
4. **Permanent ban:** a permanent ban from any interaction with the project.
